# Shure digital wireless handheld microphone (SLX-D SLXD2 / QLX-D QLXD2, SM58-style head)

Demo product. A black handheld transmitter with a round mesh grille on top, a small blue-backlit LCD and a
round power button with a status LED on the body, and a battery compartment that opens by unscrewing the
lower body (battery cup). It pairs with a separate rack/desk receiver that is wired to the speakers/mixer.
The exact variant (SLXD2 vs QLXD2) is printed on the body; if not legible, say "Shure wireless handheld" and
avoid model-only details.

## Parts on the mic
- Grille / head: microphone capsule. Speak 2–5 cm from the top, aimed at the mouth, not from the side.
- Power button: round button next to the LCD. Press and hold about 1–2 s to turn ON; press and hold again
  to turn OFF. A quick tap only wakes the display.
- Status LED (next to the power button): green/on = powered and transmitting; red = battery low;
  flashing red = battery almost dead; off = no power.
- LCD: shows the device / channel name (for example "HH-1" or an assigned name), RF/transmit info, and a
  battery gauge (bars). A lock icon means controls are locked. The backlight times out after a few seconds;
  tap power to wake it.
- IR window (small dark window near the LCD): used to sync with the receiver.
- Battery cup: unscrew the lower body counter-clockwise. Takes 2× AA (match the + / − marks) or a Shure
  rechargeable pack. Screw it back fully; a loose cup = no power.

## Battery gauge
- Full bars = several hours left. 1 bar or red LED = replace soon (under ~1 hour). Flashing red / no
  power-on = replace now. AA alkaline lasts roughly 8 hours. Fresh batteries fix most "dead mic" calls.

## Pairing (sync) with the receiver
1. Mic ON. Receiver powered (its display lit).
2. On the receiver, press SYNC (front panel; sometimes via the menu).
3. Within a few seconds, hold the mic's IR window 5–15 cm in front of the receiver's IR window, facing it.
4. Receiver shows sync success; its RF meter lights when the mic is on. The mic and receiver now share
   the same channel/frequency.
Re-sync after changing batteries is not needed; it is needed if someone changed the channel on either side
or you swapped mics between receivers.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Check / fix |
| - | - |
| Mic will not turn on, LCD blank, LED off | Hold power 2 s. Unscrew battery cup, check both AAs are in the right way (+/−), replace with fresh AAs, screw cup fully tight. |
| LED red or flashing, 1 battery bar | Replace batteries now. |
| Mic on but no sound in the room | Look at the receiver: is its RF meter lit? If no RF → mic and receiver are not paired/on the same channel: re-sync (above). If RF lit but no audio meter → speak into the top of the grille; check the receiver's audio meter moves. If the receiver meter moves but speakers are silent → receiver output cable/mixer channel/volume is the problem, not the mic. |
| Sound cuts out / crackles | Low battery first. Then distance/line of sight to the receiver (stay within ~30 m, don't cover the bottom of the mic with your hand — the antenna is in the lower body). Interference: on the receiver run a scan, then re-sync the mic. |
| Too quiet | Speak closer to the top of the grille, not the side. Gain is set on the receiver/mixer. |
| Distorted / too loud | Back off a few cm; lower the mixer channel or receiver gain. |
| Lock icon, buttons do nothing | Controls are locked from the menu (prevents accidental power-off). Unlock in the mic menu, or ask the AV tech. |
| Wrong mic name on receiver | Mic is synced to a different receiver channel; re-sync to the intended receiver. |

## Safety / limits
Never open the mic body beyond the battery cup, never use damaged batteries, never pull the grille off
with force. If the mic won't power on with fresh batteries, or won't sync after two tries at close range,
hand off to an AV technician / swap the mic.
